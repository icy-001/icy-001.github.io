<b>Bob</b>: What makes someone a nice person?

<b>Alice</b>: I'm a nice person.

<b>Bob</b>: What makes you say that?

<b>Alice</b>: Ask any of my friends. I'm always nice to them.

<b>Bob</b>: What about Charlie?

<b>Alice</b>: Charlie's an asshole. He deserves it.

<b>Bob</b>: Is Charlie nice?

<b>Alice</b>: No. Weren't you listening? He's an asshole.

<b>Bob</b>: Charlie, are you nice?

<b>Charlie</b>: Yes! Ask any of my friends. I'm always nice to them.
